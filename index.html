ABOUT UNIBOND

Connecting the right requirement with the right technology.

Unibond Technologies is a technology provider, consulting and solution-partnering company focused on government, infrastructure and technology-led projects. Our aim is to connect the right requirement with the right technology and the right set of partners.

We work across different solution areas and engage with OEMs, software companies, system integrators, implementation agencies and other industry partners depending on the needs of a project. Instead of being limited to one product or technology, we focus on understanding the opportunity and building a suitable solution ecosystem around it.

Our approach is simple - understand the requirement, identify the right technology and partners, and help take the opportunity forward in a practical and commercially workable manner.
